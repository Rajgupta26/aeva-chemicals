TEXTILE AUXILIARIES / 01

AeSop

Low-temperature soaping agent

Clean shades.
Clear advantage.

Polymer wash-off chemistry for reactive-dyed cotton. AeSop removes loose dye and helps keep it from redepositing, so shades stay bright and white grounds stay clean.

SUPPLIED AS Concentrated liquid

01

Cleaner white grounds

Helps prevent hydrolysed reactive dye from staining adjacent white fabric.

02

Low-temperature wash-off

Works from 60 °C, with a starting process at 70 °C subject to plant trial.

03

Low foam in circulation

Surfactant-free chemistry designed for jet, soft-flow and winch machines.

APPLICATION GUIDANCE

A starting point
for your process.

  1. 01

    Rinse & neutralise

    Drop the dyebath, cold rinse, then neutralise with acetic acid at 50 °C.

  2. 02

    Dose & soap

    Dose AeSop in a fresh bath. The brochure starting recipe is 70 °C for 20 minutes.

  3. 03

    Rinse & finish

    Drain and warm rinse at 50 °C before proceeding to softening.
