About Aeva · Inside the soaping bath

  1. 01 · After dyeing

    Loose dye clings to the cotton.

    Reactive dyeing never fixes every molecule. The rest sits on the fibre, ready to bleed in the first wash and stain the whites beside it.

  2. 02 · Soaping at 60–70 °C

    AeSop lifts it off.

    Our polymer wash-off chemistry pulls the loose dye away from the fibre at 60–70 °C, rather than in a near-boiling bath.

  3. 03 · In the liquor

    Then holds it there.

    The polymer keeps the dye suspended in the water, so it drains away instead of settling back on the cloth.

  4. 04 · Out of the bath

    Bright shades. Clean whites.

    That is what Aeva Chemicals is for: a Mumbai team making soaping and finishing chemistry for dyehouses in India and abroad.
